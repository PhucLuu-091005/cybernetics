Event tracker design system

Direction D2 Radix calm, drawn flat with few cards. Geist type, Radix colour steps, hairlines over boxes. Colour shows status or change; grey shows everything else. The text version with every rule is docs/design-system.md.

1. Principles

  1. Modern, quiet, flat. It must feel like Linear or Cursor: grey surfaces, one ink colour for text, colour only where it carries meaning.
  2. Few cards. The rail, the Queue, drawer lists, history and review lists use bold text and hairlines. No box, no radius, no shadow.
  3. Only floating layers are surfaces. Modal, pop, popover menu, drawer, toast and the event brief get a panel, a border, a radius and a shadow.
  4. Colour means status or change. Blue, green, red, amber and teal show status. Yellow shows a change in a change request. Grey shows selection.
  5. Plain English UI words. "Move the date", "Hand over", "Not now", "Show again".
  6. Every drag has a click way, and every SVG target is a labelled button.

7. Graph marks

Larkspur names and milestone dates at the demo clock, 9 Oct 18:00; the row states are a sample. The view box is 1060 units wide; the label column is 228 units; rows are 32 units. No connectors: a child sits indented under its parent, and "leads from" shows only as a handoff mark.

Rows from the top: a selected row (grey band), an open row past its planned finish with a keyboard focus ring, a done row with a 2-receiver handoff mark, a changed row with a yellow highlighter, an indented child that waits, a row whose source is hidden (branch chip), and a row finished late.

Each mark

Graph rules

  1. No connector: no line from the milestone line to a row, no parent to child line, no "leads from" curve, no ghost line for a hidden source.
  2. One handoff mark per source row lists every receiver. A click jumps to the receiver, or opens a short list when there are several.
  3. The axis band (dates, T pill, milestone line) stays pinned while the rows scroll. Scrolls stop below it.
  4. A hidden row or team leaves no row, no line and no link. A team without shown rows takes no room, except an empty team, which keeps its lane as a drop target.
  5. An event sits at its time plus your own day offset, inside its day and in time order.
  6. No chip column at the row end. The deadline diamond tells the milestone.
  7. Main and any view you cannot edit show information only. The sideways move inside a day works everywhere.
  8. Every SVG target is role="button" with tabindex="0", an aria-label, and Enter or Space.

8. Accessibility

  • Contrast: text 4.5:1 and lines 3:1 in both themes (see the contrast table). Status always has a word or an icon too.
  • Focus: :focus-visible with a 2px --focus outline, offset 2px. Inputs use the border and ring style. Drawers, pops and modal cards take the focus without a ring; their controls keep theirs.
  • Toggles: aria-pressed on buttons that stay on (segments, Full view, focus button). aria-haspopup and aria-expanded on buttons that open a list.
  • Esc order: an open menu, the search list or the handoff list closes itself first. Then the modal, the move mode, the pop, the drawer, the focus, and last the full view. One Esc closes one layer.
  • Keyboard: every drag has a click way; the search list works with arrows, Enter and Esc.
  • Phone: no horizontal page scroll at 390px; the graph scrolls inside its canvas.

9. Never

  • No dashed or dotted lines, anywhere: graph, borders, dividers, focus rings, option pages.
  • No pink, magenta or violet change marks. Change is yellow.
  • No coloured edge stripes on cards, rows or callouts.
  • No status colour for selection, hover or focus. Selection is a grey ink band. Focus is --focus.
  • No cards for the rail, the Queue or drawer lists. Bold text and hairlines.
  • No connector lines on the graph.
  • No new colour, radius, shadow or font size outside the tokens. Add a token in both themes first.
  • No jargon in UI text. Plain English, one word for one action.
  • No hard-coded hex colours in components, except #fff text on member avatars.
