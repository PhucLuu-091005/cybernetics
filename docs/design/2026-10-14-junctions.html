Junction symbols

Children stay in their own department lanes (option 1). Where work leaves the promise, returns to it, or moves from one department to the next, a symbol on the promise line says so. Development no longer forks four times from four places: it fans out from one handoff junction.

Where the symbols come from. Split and return come from the parent and child dates; they need no new data. The handoff symbol ("Analysts → Engineering") needs to know that development waited for the analysis: either the app infers it (a child closes and children of another department start within 2 business days) or the agent records it (an after link, option 2).
