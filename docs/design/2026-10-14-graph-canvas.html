Graph canvas: mockup for review

The graph becomes a canvas panel, as in Miro, draw.io and Figma. Try: scroll to move, Shift + scroll to move sideways, Ctrl/⌘ + scroll or pinch to zoom around the pointer, drag empty space to move, drag a dot (it stays in its day and lane), double-click a branch to zoom to that loop, the % menu for presets, the map button for the minimap, and the last button for full screen. Loop titles appear from 60 % and source labels from 80 %. Zoom to a loop stops at 150 %.
